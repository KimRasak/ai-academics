\title{When World Models Become Too Literal: Three-Axis Mechanistic Localization and High-Noise Adaptation for 2D Non-Physical Anime Grammar in Flagship Video DiTs}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Scaling open-weight Image-to-Video Diffusion Transformers (Video DiTs) from 5B to flagship 13B--27B Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) scales resolves classic 3D physical kinematics on 2D anime characters, including smooth head turns, dynamic wind, and even $180^\circ$ occlusion-and-return. Yet we uncover a sharp \emph{Flagship Scaling Paradox}: that same strong 3D physical world-model prior systematically breaks \textbf{2D Non-Physical Anime Visual Grammar}---stylized graphic signifiers that intentionally violate real-world optics and anatomy, such as specular eye-highlight extinction in daylight, translucent upper-forehead gloom shadows with bright hair and calm smiles, popping temple anger veins, and rigid sliding comic sweat drops. Across \textbf{four independent flagship families} spanning \textbf{four distinct flow schedules} (\texttt{Wan2.2-I2V-A14B} 27B MoE, \texttt{LTX-Video-0.9.7} 13B 48L non-MoE, \texttt{HunyuanVideo-I2V} 13B MM-DiT, and \texttt{HunyuanVideo-1.5} 54L MM-DiT), base models suffer universal \emph{Physical World-Model Literalism}---replacing 2D signifiers with full-room lighting blackouts, eyelid-closing escapes, 3D plastic hairclips, and melting liquid slime---while training-free prompt engineering, negative prompting, and CFG tuning are trapped in a \emph{Literalism vs.\ Refusal Double-Bind}. Opening the denoising trajectory reveals a \textbf{Three-Axis Mechanistic Localization} of 2D visual grammar: (1) a universal \emph{noise-space law} ($\sigma \ge 0.80$) that holds across MoE and monolithic architectures regardless of schedule shift, with the bottom 80\% of noise ($\sigma < 0.80$) yielding zero semantic leverage; (2) velocity-field rewiring in value/output/FFN projections rather than cross-attention $Q\cdot K$ sharpening; and (3) a \emph{cross-architecture depth law} where the final third of transformer blocks is universally semantically dead, while active grammar crystallizes in the earliest multimodal blocks of MM-DiT/MoE models versus the middle third of deep monolithic DiTs. Intersecting all three axes compresses adaptation to just \textbf{0.017\%--0.024\%} (4.6M--6.6M parameters) of a 27B DiT with zero retraining, while our clean foreground-clipped self-bootstrapped adaptation cures all flagship families with zero forgetting on photorealistic humans, generalizing zero-shot to unseen 3D+2D compositions, unseen art styles, complex multi-lighting scenes, 5-second temporal horizons, and brand-new unseen manga symbols.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Frontier Image-to-Video Diffusion Transformers (Video DiTs) have scaled to 13B--27B parameters with sophisticated flow-matching schedules, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) denoising specialists, and multimodal vision-language encoders. As we verify empirically, scaling from 5B to 27B largely solves traditional 3D physical motion failure modes on 2D anime portraits: 27B models smoothly rotate anime characters through $180^\circ$ back-of-head occlusions and recover their original identity with fidelity nearly indistinguishable from photorealistic humans.

However, 2D animation is \emph{not} merely a flat-shaded rendering of 3D physics. Japanese anime and manga rely on a rich \textbf{2D Non-Physical Visual Grammar}---iconic visual signifiers that deliberately break physical optics, illumination, and anatomy to convey affect. A character experiencing sudden dread in bright daylight can lose only the white specular highlight dots in wide-open colored irises, or acquire a translucent hatched gloom band across the upper forehead while their bright hair, warm smile, and sunlit room remain completely untouched. Similarly, irritation pops a stylized flat four-bracket anger vein onto the temple, and awkwardness slides a rigid cel-shaded comic sweat drop beside the brow.

We demonstrate that across \textbf{four independent flagship open-weight Video DiT families} (\texttt{Wan2.2-I2V-A14B} 27B MoE, \texttt{LTX-Video-0.9.7} 13B non-MoE, \texttt{HunyuanVideo-I2V} 13B MM-DiT, and \texttt{HunyuanVideo-1.5} 54L MM-DiT), strong 3D physical priors enforce \textbf{Physical World-Model Literalism}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \emph{Illumination literalism}: ``dark forehead shadow'' triggers global room blackout or opaque cardboard/hair-dye occlusion instead of translucent 2D gloom.
    \item \emph{Anatomical literalism}: ``eye highlights vanish'' triggers eyelid closure or glowing demon eyes instead of specular-only extinction.
    \item \emph{Object literalism}: ``red anger vein'' becomes a 3D plastic hairclip or bleeding cut; ``comic sweat drop'' melts into gravity-obeying blue slime or crying tears.
\end{itemize}
Crucially, we show that best-effort training-free interventions (strong anti-literalism negative prompts, hyper-explicit 2D prompts, and high/low CFG) fail due to a structural \textbf{Training-Free Double-Bind}: raising CFG amplifies physical literalism, while negating physical objects eliminates the frozen world model's sole generative pathway and induces refusal or bleaching.

Our contributions are fourfold:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Deconfounded 4-Family Flagship Benchmark}: A 100\% open-weight, zero-horror-vocabulary benchmark separating solved 3D kinematics from universally failing 2D non-physical anime grammar across four 13B--27B architectures and four flow schedules.
    \item \textbf{Three-Axis Mechanistic Localization}: Causal MoE swapping, fine-grained noise-space sweeps, post-adaptation attention/velocity probing, and zero-retraining layer-depth slicing pinpointing 2D semantic grammar to $\sigma \ge 0.80$ noise levels, V/O/FFN velocity projections, and early/mid multimodal blocks (with universal final-third semantic deadness across architectures).
    \item \textbf{Clean Self-Bootstrapped High-Noise Adaptation}: A foreground-clipped, specular-preserving synthetic video adaptation pipeline that cures flagship architectures while preserving photorealistic human motion ($16/16$ exact ties), verified across 3 random seeds and dual independent 32B VLM judges.
    \item \textbf{Extreme Sparsity \& Zero-Shot Generalization}: Intersecting all three mechanistic axes yields statistically significant grammar adaptation in just $0.017\%$--$0.024\%$ of a 27B model, while full adaptation generalizes zero-shot to unseen 3D+2D compositions, unseen art styles and generators, complex multi-lighting scenes, $1.65\times$ longer horizons (81 frames), simultaneous multi-symbol compositions, and brand-new unseen manga graphic overlays.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mechanistic Framework: Three-Axis Localization}
Let a flow-matching Video DiT $v_\theta(x_\sigma, \sigma, c, x_0^{\text{img}})$ predict velocity $v = \epsilon - x_0$ along $x_\sigma = (1 - \sigma) x_0 + \sigma \epsilon$ with schedule-specific time shifting $\sigma(u) = \frac{s \cdot u}{1 + (s - 1) u}$ (e.g., $s=3.0$ for \texttt{Wan2.2}, $s=7.0$ for \texttt{HunyuanVideo-1.5}, $s=17.0$ for \texttt{HunyuanVideo-I2V}, or \texttt{linear\_quadratic} for \texttt{LTX-Video}).

\subsection{Axis 1: Universal Noise-Space Threshold ($\sigma \ge 0.80$)}
Comparing MoE expert boundaries ($t \ge 900$ in \texttt{Wan2.2}) against monolithic schedules reveals that step index alone is confounded by schedule shift $s$. Under $s=17.0$ (\texttt{HunyuanVideo-I2V}), the first half of steps ($0\dots 14$) lies entirely in $\sigma \in [0.951, 1.000]$, above the secondary refinement band. Evaluating directly in noise space $\sigma$ unifies all architectures:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bottom 80\% noise ($\sigma < 0.80$)}: Zero causal effect on 2D semantic grammar across all tested backbones ($p=1.0$ vs.\ frozen base).
    \item \textbf{Two-stage high-noise formation ($\sigma \ge 0.80$)}: On monolithic \texttt{LTX-13B}, $\sigma \in [0.98, 1.00]$ breaks global blackout and places a coarse spatial scaffold, while just six steps in $\sigma \in [0.95, 0.98]$ produce a $7.3\times$ jump into translucent vertical-hatched 2D gloom (96\% of full adaptation).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Axis 2: Velocity-Field Rewiring ($V/O/\text{FFN}$ vs.\ $Q\cdot K$)}
Probing cross-attention maps and Classifier-Free Guidance velocity residuals $\Delta v_\sigma = v_\theta(x_\sigma, \sigma, c) - v_\theta(x_\sigma, \sigma, \varnothing)$ before and after High-Noise LoRA adaptation reveals that trigger-token $Q\cdot K$ upper-face attention mass remains essentially invariant ($\approx 0.30$), whereas off-target CFG velocity leakage drops by $25\%$ ($0.798 \to 0.601$) and lower-face velocity drops by $30\%$, cutting smile blackout by $99.5\%$. Adaptation therefore rewires the semantic-to-latent velocity mapping ($V/O/\text{FFN}$) prior to low-noise refinement rather than sharpening spatial attention heatmaps.

\subsection{Axis 3: Cross-Architecture Depth Localization}
Masking trained LoRA adapters to disjoint depth thirds at inference with zero retraining exposes a universal law and an architectural split:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Universal final-third deadness}: Across \texttt{Wan2.2-27B} (blocks 26--39), \texttt{LTX-13B} (blocks 32--47), and \texttt{HunyuanVideo-13B} (single-stream blocks 20--39), the final third produces no statistically significant semantic gain ($p \ge 0.50$), confirming that late blocks act purely as texture decoders.
    \item \textbf{Architecture-specific active bottleneck}: Semantic grammar peaks in the earliest multimodal blocks for Dual-Stream MM-DiT (\texttt{HunyuanVideo-13B} dual-stream 0--19: $90.6\%, p=0.0010$) and High-Noise MoE (\texttt{Wan2.2-27B} 0--13: $78.1\%, p=0.0117$), whereas in deep 48-layer monolithic cross-attention (\texttt{LTX-13B}) it shifts to the middle third (blocks 16--31: $71.9\%$ overall; early 0--15 dead at $p=1.0$).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Clean Self-Bootstrapped Adaptation}
To isolate authentic 2D graphic grammar from synthetic-data artifacts discovered during initial visual auditing (background shadow spill and giant-eye dark voids), our Clean v2 data pipeline constructs 176 training clips across 32 strictly disjoint characters (\texttt{fc\_08..fc\_39}, holding out 16 unseen test characters plus 12 OOD style/scene portraits):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Foreground-clipped gloom}: Border flood-fill segmentation strictly confines translucent vertical-hatched gloom inside the character silhouette, eliminating studio-background bleed.
    \item \textbf{Specular-only highlight extinction}: Localized peak-luminance masking inside detected iris regions removes only specular dots while preserving matte iris color and bright sclera.
    \item \textbf{3D kinematics anchors (20\%)}: Base-model 3D head-turn and wind clips anchor photorealistic and anime 3D motion, ensuring zero catastrophic forgetting.
\end{itemize}
Adapting solely the high-noise flow regime (or full flow with inference-time $\sigma \ge 0.80$ gating) via low-rank flow matching ($r \in \{4, 16, 32\}$) requires only $0.017\%$--$0.35\%$ of flagship parameters.
