Composite automatic metrics decide leaderboard positions for text-to-video models, yet their sensitivity to the generator seed is rarely quantified. We reproduce the released Sci-VBench VT results for MiniMax-H3 to 10^-6 and then generate 16 prompts 12 seeds for three architecturally different models (MiniMax-H3, Wan2.1-Fun, LTX-Video-2B; 576 videos) under the released protocol. The within-prompt across-seed SD of VT is 0.051 for H3, so the paired SE of a single-seed two-model mean difference (0.035 at 95\%) is 5.3 the 0.0066 median gap between adjacent released models. The binary dynamic-degree (DD) component flips in 27.5\% of H3 seed pairs (CI [24.9,30.2]\%), and in model-specific but always substantial rates on Wan (17.2\%) and LTX (11.9\%); the rate is invariant to any binarization threshold in [0.05,0.60]. Prompt and seed variance shares are nearly equal in all three models (ANOVA ICC_prompt 0.46--0.56). A descriptive join with 2,392 human expert scores shows pooled rank correlation -0.04 between VT and expert LPF (cluster-bootstrap CI [-0.09,+0.01]). Single-seed leaderboard comparisons are not resolvable at released gaps; we recommend multi-seed reporting and seed-resolved releases.
