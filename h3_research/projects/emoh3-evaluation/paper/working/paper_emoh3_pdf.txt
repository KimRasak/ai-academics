MiniMax H3. We showed that (i) current evalua-
tion is unstable along steps, seeds, and judge frame
counts, and must be swept to be trustworthy; (ii) a
reference-conditioned LoRA trained on cross-emotion
pairs makes the first negative emotion (neutral→sad)
expressible, with a controlled neutral baseline and
seed-dependent success, and improves positive-target
seed robustness; and (iii) a specific class of strong-
negative emotions remains a hard boundary across
all interventions, with a controlled resolution abla-
tion ruling out the layout-mismatch explanation. We
release the full verdict matrix, judge logs, and check-
points to support honest benchmarking of emotion
control in open video generators.
